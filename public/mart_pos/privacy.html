Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Mart POS ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our point-of-sale application.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Business Information (company name, phone number)
  • User Account Information (name, role, phone number)
  • Product Information (inventory, prices)
  • Sales Transaction Data
  • Currency Settings

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Managing your business inventory
  • Processing sales transactions
  • Creating backup copies of your data
  • Providing technical support
  • Improving our services

Data Storage and Security

Your data is primarily stored locally on your device using SQLite database. We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information:

  • Secure local database storage
  • Automated backup systems
  • Role-based access control
  • Encrypted data transmission during sync operations

Data Backup and Sync

Our application includes features for:

  • Automatic data backup to our secure servers
  • Incremental backups to minimize data transfer
  • Data restoration capabilities
  • Account synchronization across devices

User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored information
  • Update your business and personal information
  • Request data backup copies
  • Delete your account and associated data

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Digital Ocean for server hosting
  • WhatsApp for customer support

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
